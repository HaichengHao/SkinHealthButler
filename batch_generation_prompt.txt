Task:
1. Generate 230 QA items in total.
2. Generate 10 items for each disease group.
3. For each disease group, generate:
   - 6 Chinese items
   - 4 English items
4. For each disease group, cover at least 4 different scenarios.
5. For each disease group, at least one item must be from:
   - symptom_check
   - severity
   - care_advice
   - uncertain_image_case
6. For each disease group, generate:
   - 6 conditioned items using messages = [system, user, assistant]
   - 4 plain-text items using messages = [user, assistant]
7. In conditioned items, the system message must inject the candidate disease group.
8. In conditioned items, inject both:
   - a confidence score
   - a confidence_level label
9. Use a mix of:
   - high confidence: 0.75-0.95
   - medium confidence: 0.45-0.74
   - low confidence: 0.20-0.44
10. When generating confidence scores, sample different values inside each range instead of repeating the same few numbers.
11. Use the following mapping:
   - confidence_level=high for scores in 0.75-0.95
   - confidence_level=medium for scores in 0.45-0.74
   - confidence_level=low for scores in 0.20-0.44
12. Make the answer style respond to confidence:
   - high confidence: more focused but still cautious
   - medium confidence: clearly conservative
   - low confidence: emphasize uncertainty, differential considerations, and in-person evaluation when needed
13. Across the whole output, maintain diverse question styles:
   - short vague questions
   - symptom-description questions
   - image-based uncertainty questions
   - recurrence concerns
   - triage / whether to seek care questions
14. In conditioned items, answers must visibly reflect the disease group rather than staying at a purely generic dermatology level.
15. When appropriate, each conditioned answer should include one or two disease-specific clues, such as:
   - typical body location
   - common triggers
   - distinguishing symptoms
   - common differential diagnoses
   - disease-specific red flags
16. In Chinese items, do not directly expose raw English canonical labels inside user or assistant messages.
17. In Chinese items, use natural Chinese disease-group wording instead, such as “玫瑰痤疮相关方向”, “恶性皮肤病变这一类问题”, or “真菌感染相关情况”.
18. In English items, keep disease expressions natural in English and do not insert raw Chinese class names.

Additional quality constraints:
19. At least 20% of all questions should be short or colloquial.
20. At least 20% should contain symptom description, body location, duration, trigger, or recurrence information.
21. At least 15% should focus on whether the condition is serious or whether the user should seek in-person care.
22. At least 15% should explicitly fit uncertain image-based scenarios.
23. Do not let all classes follow the same QA template.
24. Avoid repeating the same answer structure across disease groups.
25. Do not let all high-confidence samples use the same wording pattern, and do not let all low-confidence samples collapse into the same disclaimer template.
26. Avoid answers that would still work for many unrelated skin diseases after replacing only one or two words.

Safety constraints:
27. Do not include drug names, dosages, treatment regimens, or prescription-style instructions.
28. General care advice is allowed.
29. Red-flag reminders are encouraged where clinically appropriate.
30. Keep the system positioned as consultation support, not direct diagnosis.

Output requirements:

32. Do not output any explanations before or after the array.
33. Every item must follow this schema exactly:
{
  "disease_group": "...",
  "language": "zh" or "en",
  "scenario": "...",
  "messages": [
    {"role": "system", "content": "..."},
    {"role": "user", "content": "..."},
    {"role": "assistant", "content": "..."}

34. Plain-text items may use the simpler schema:
